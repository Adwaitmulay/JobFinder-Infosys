# JobFinder career-source policy

JobFinder stores jobs only from official company career pages/career systems.

Current demo source:
- Company: Infosys
- Official careers listing: https://digitalcareers.infosys.com/infosys/global-careers
- Official job detail domain: https://digitalcareers.infosys.com/
- Source type: official_company_careers

Do not import jobs from Naukri, Indeed, Unstop, TimesJobs, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, or other third-party job boards.

When a new company is added, its official careers page must be added as a first-party collector before jobs from that company are imported.
